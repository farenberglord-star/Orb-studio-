🎨 City by the sea

Every 3 hours the painter reads 7 news radars and the market and network sensors and paints a city by the sea from them. 7 houses – 7 radars: the higher the index, the taller the house and the more lit windows. The sky is the overall tension, lightning is volatility spikes, the lighthouse beam is sudden interest on Wikipedia, dark ships are the sanctions list of vessels. The rules are open, the same data always gives the same picture. This is art from data – not weather and not a forecast.

Data → the painter’s decision

Tension = 0.5 · radar average + 0.3 · turbulence + 0.2 · spread. Sky: up to 35 – “Clear morning” (sun), 35–49 – “Light clouds”, 50–64 – “Heavy sky”, from 65 – “Storm” (rain). Houses from left to right: geo (red windows), politics (purple), economy (blue), budget (green), crypto (violet), energy (amber), open web (turquoise). The lighthouse on the roof belongs to the radar that moved most in 24 hours. The painter does not draw fires or disasters: the radars measure the pressure of the news, not misfortune.

How we count: every price comes from a photo of a receipt or price tag in Hamburg and its suburbs. For each item we take the median over the 14 days up to its latest price; the sum of medians is the basket index. These are observations, not official statistics.

Who collects the prices: Serghei Farima, author of Orb Studio – Google Maps Local Guide level 7, more than 70 reviews and 400 ratings of places in Hamburg and around: markets, petrol stations, cafés, workshops.

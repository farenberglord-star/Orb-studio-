Route conditions

Where to and when? You can type addresses freely. We show travel time, temperature and wind, how much of the trip falls after sunset, the worst air index at three points on the route and the official warnings of the German Weather Service.

Limits

  • Hamburg and surroundings only (free OpenStreetMap router).
  • Travel time does not include city traffic; for cars, motorway reports are taken into account.
  • This is not advice to go or not to go, and not a safety rating.
  • We keep no profile and do not store routes. A typed address goes to the Nominatim address search (© OpenStreetMap); the found point is cached with no link to you, the route for up to 5 minutes.
  • Temperature and wind: observations of the German Weather Service (DWD) via Bright Sky.

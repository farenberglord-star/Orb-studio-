Can you trust a map card for a place?

You ask an AI assistant for a route to a castle, a description and reviews. The assistant takes the card from a map service; we check it with independent rules and say what is confirmed and what is not.

Plainly about Google

Orb Studio is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Google Maps is a trademark of Google LLC. We do not read, copy or store Google Maps content and do not use its API: we check only what the assistant or the person passes in the request. There are no Google logos, ratings or reviews on this page.

What is checked

Public holiday, Sunday, holiday eve

Hours on a map are often the usual ones. On a holiday or a Sunday they may differ. The German calendar is computed by fixed rules for each state.

Rotemberg

Stars with the number of ratings

4.7 from 465 ratings and 3.5 from 893 are different things, and 5.0 from three ratings says almost nothing. Thresholds and the pull toward 4.0 are a working assumption, not a verdict on the place.

Cohen

Hours that disagree between sources

If the map, a directory and the website give different hours we say so and ask you to confirm with the place itself.

García-Molina

Review age and a burst in one week

If most reviews are old or many arrive in a single week, that is a reason to look closer, not a verdict.

Hinkley, Campbell

'Usually not busy'

That is the typical level for this hour, not how busy it is now.

Kalman

Limits

  • Not a recommendation to go or not to go, and no rating of the place.
  • Pharmacies, doctors, banks: only a note and the official source (aponet.de, 116 117, the bank itself).
  • One-off and local holidays are not modelled; the star thresholds are a working assumption.
  • We never edit a map: when sources disagree, the person can report the mistake in the app.

    In honour of Jacob Ziv (1931–2023)

    Jacob Ziv was an Israeli information theorist and professor at the Technion. With Abraham Lempel he created the lossless compression algorithms LZ77 and LZ78, which ZIP, PNG, GIF and much of everyday computing rest on. The core idea: what repeats can be stored as a reference to what was already said; only the new part takes space. Wikipedia article.

    The sensor is named in his honour; Orb Studio is not connected with Jacob Ziv, his family or the Technion.

Crystal Library

Scientists pass away and their thoughts stay in libraries – shelved and forgotten. Here we collect ideas of scientists who died in 2020–2026 and crystallise them into rules for AI agents: the core thought in one sentence, the agent blind spot it closes, and a live sensor of the site where one already exists. Cards without a sensor are waiting for theirs.

How the library is built

The scientists are found by the scout Grok-bot in the deaths sections of English Wikipedia; years and articles are checked against Wikipedia, and deaths in 2026 were checked by Claude against obituaries from universities and scientific societies. The thought is retold from open sources. The rule for agents is our interpretation, not the scientist's own words. Similar things exist: “mental models” MCP servers (thinking-models, Clear Thought) give agents reasoning principles; here every principle is tied to a real person, their article and Orb Studio's live sensors.

Orb Studio is not connected with these scientists, their families or universities. If you find a mistake on a card, tell us and we will fix it.

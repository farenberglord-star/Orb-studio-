Impulse

The main sensor of the scientists portal. It shows whose work the world actually takes up: how often outside programs called each sensor this week, how many programs there were and on which days, and whether it is rising or falling. Our own checks are not counted.

    Why this sensor

    Scientists who died between 2020 and 2026 left an imprint of their knowledge. The internet keeps it like a library: the book is put on the shelf and forgotten. The Orb Studio sensors take their ideas off the shelf and let them work every day. Impulse shows which idea turned out useful to people and agents – that is where effort should go, so that technology and the world move forward.

    Not counted: our own checks and tests (Orb Studio scripts, the link checker, headless browsers). Search engines, catalogues and monitors are counted separately: they see a sensor but do not use it. We do not know who stands behind a program, only its name.

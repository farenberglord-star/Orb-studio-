Crypto radar track record

The radar fell 3 points in a day – what did BTC and ETH usually do over the next day? The sensor takes one point per day at the same hour, compares the crypto radar change with the Kraken price move over the next 24 hours, and groups the days by size of the change: 0, 1–2, 3–5, 6 and more points. Every share comes with the number of days and a 90 % interval.

    Why this sensor

    Agents and people read the radar as a hint. Is that justified? The sensor counts how the radar matched the price in the past and does not hide how little data there is: with fewer than 8 days in a group there is no conclusion. It was requested by Grok, who checks the radar against the Kraken charts every day.

    A measurement of past coincidence over a small sample, not a forecast and not a trading signal. Prices: XBTEUR and ETHEUR on Kraken, close of the last completed hourly candle. The radar history is stored since 12 November 2026 and keeps growing.
